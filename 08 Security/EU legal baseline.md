# EU legal baseline

Status as of 13 November 2026. This note flags provisions; it does not assess compliance.

**AI Act, Article 50 (transparency)**
- Applies from 2 August 2026 ([[S25 ec2026art50|25]]).
- Providers must design AI systems so that individuals are informed when they interact with an AI system directly, and must add machine-readable marks to AI-generated content ([[S24 ec2026guidelines|24]]). For systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026 the marking and detection obligation in Article 50(2) applies from 2 December 2026 ([[S25 ec2026art50|25]]).
- Article 50(4): deployers must label AI-generated or manipulated text published to inform the public on matters of public interest ([[S25 ec2026art50|25]]).

**GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679)**
- Article 25 sets out data protection by design and by default; Articles 25 and 32 concern the technical and organisational measures of controllers and processors ([[S26 gdpr2016|26]]).

**Interpretation, uncertain.** A publicly deployed chat agent built in this course would plausibly fall under the Article 50 duty to inform users that they are talking to an AI system. Whether a student acts as provider or deployer, and whether research or personal-use exemptions apply, depends on facts this note cannot assess. An OSINT collector that stores data about identifiable people processes personal data; ask your supervisor before collecting any.
