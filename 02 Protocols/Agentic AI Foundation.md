# Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF)

- Formed under the Linux Foundation on 9 December 2025 with three founding contributions: Anthropic's MCP, Block's goose and OpenAI's AGENTS.md ([[S46 lf2025aaif|46]]).
- On 22 October 2026 four projects joined: Agent Skills (Anthropic), Open Responses (OpenAI), OpenEnv (Hugging Face), OpenSandbox (Alibaba) ([[S47 lf2026aaif|47]]).
- Project list at that date: A2A, Agent Router, agentgateway, AGENTS.md, goose, MCP, plus the four new ones ([[S47 lf2026aaif|47]]).

**Interpretation.** Instructions ([[AGENTS.md]]), capabilities ([[SKILL.md]]), tools ([[MCP]]) and agent-to-agent traffic ([[A2A]]) now sit under one vendor-neutral body. Building on them lowers lock-in risk for a project that must outlive one tool.
