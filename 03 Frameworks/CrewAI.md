# CrewAI

- **Flows** are structured, event-driven workflows that manage state and control execution; **Crews** are teams of autonomous role-playing agents that a Flow delegates to ([[S22 crewai_docs|22]]).
- Typical run: the Flow manages state, hands a complex task to a Crew, receives the result and continues ([[S22 crewai_docs|22]]).
- Repository: 60,832 stars, MIT ([[S29 github2026meta|29]]).

**Interpretation.** The closest fit to the "team of analysts" mental model, which makes it readable for CI students. The Flow layer is where you keep determinism. See [[MAS roles]].
